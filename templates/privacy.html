Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you create an account, such as your name and email address, and the expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type, to keep the service working.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate Spendly: to show your expenses, produce summaries and breakdowns, and keep your account secure. We do not sell your personal data or use your expense details for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database and protected with reasonable safeguards. We keep it for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not share your expense data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data deleted, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
